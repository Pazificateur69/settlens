# Privacy notice.

Last updated: 31 July 2026.

### Controller

Settlens is currently a pre-incorporation project. Before accepting production customer data, publish the operating entity’s full legal name, registered address and privacy contact here.

### Data we collect

For pilot requests, we collect the name, work email, company and message you provide. Scan reports contain technical evidence and do not require private keys or custody of funds.

### Use and retention

We use these details to respond to requests and provide assurance reports. Confirm retention periods and subprocessors with counsel before launch.

### Your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or export of personal data through our [contact form](contact.html).
